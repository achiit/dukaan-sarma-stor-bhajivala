Our story

My father's recipe, still on the same flame

My father, Mohanbhai, opened this small shop in 1998 with two kadhais and one rule: never sell what you would not feed your own children. I used to sit on a sack of sugar and watch him stir.

Today I do the stirring. The ghee still comes from the same dairy, and the ladoos are still rolled by hand. Many customers now bring their own children, and that is the best compliment we get.

  • Made fresh every morning, never stored for days
  • Pure ghee, with no palm oil blends
  • Same family, same recipes since 1998
  • Clean open kitchen you are welcome to see
  • Bulk and festival orders packed on time
